## Decision: BlocConsumer over BlocBuilder 
- **Decision**: `QuizPage` logic relies on `BlocConsumer`.
- **Rationale**: `BlocBuilder` only redraws the UI. It cannot execute navigation triggers cleanly. `BlocConsumer` allows us to both draw the Loading screen and listen for the `QuizLoaded` trigger simultaneously, letting us push to `ResultPage` cleanly.
